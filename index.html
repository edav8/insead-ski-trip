01 — The trip

Seven days off the grid, on the glacier.

Once a year the cohort swaps Fontainebleau for altitude. Everyone on the same mountain, in the same village, on the same lift pass — beginners included. You do not need to be good at skiing. You need to be there.

04 — Before you ask

FAQ.

I have never skied. Genuinely never.

Perfect. A large chunk of the cohort hasn't either. Beginner lessons run every morning and the beginner group is the loudest group on the trip.

Do I need my own gear?

No. Take the gear add-on and everything is waiting for you at the shop in the village. Bring your own gloves, goggles and thermals — those are personal.

Can I bring a partner or a friend from outside INSEAD?

Usually yes, subject to spots. Flag it on the interest form so the organisers can count beds.

What if I can only come for part of the week?

Say so on the form. Partial stays are handled case by case and priced accordingly.

Is this confirmed?

Not yet. This page exists to size demand. Registering interest costs nothing and commits you to nothing — it just tells the organisers how many beds to hold.
